Young Democrats of South Carolina

International Committee

The Young Democrats of South Carolina International Committee seeks to represent South Carolina and the Democratic Party positively on the global stage. We build relationships with international youth political organizations and coalitions that share our values. Our long-term goals being to foster international collaboration, develop future leaders, promote South Carolina’s maritime interests, and establish a pipeline for greater state involvement in international relations. So we hope you join us in international solidarity.


The International Committee is organized into two complementary wings: the Delegate Wing and the Minister Wing. Members may serve in either or both, according to their responsibilities and expertise. Membership in the two wings is not mutually exclusive.
