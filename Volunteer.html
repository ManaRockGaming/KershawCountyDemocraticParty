Who Are We?
The Kershaw
County
Democratic Party

We are the people of Kershaw County, from Camden, Elgin, Bethune, and every community in between. These are the places that shaped us, raised us, and gave us a home. We believe South Carolina is worth fighting for.

Whether you're a lifelong Democrat, a liberal, a progressive, or a conservative who simply wants a better future for our community, there is a place for you here. From young people casting their first vote to working adults building their lives, we’re bringing a new Carolina into existence.

Kershaw County has deep roots, a proud history, and neighbors who show up for one another. We’re carrying that spirit forward, organizing our communities, registering voters, supporting Democratic candidates, and making sure every voice has a seat at the table.
