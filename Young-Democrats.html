Become A Dues Paying YDSC Member

For $35 annually you can become a due paying member of the Kershaw County Young Democrats and South Carolina Young Democrats. Dues paying members get a YDSC/KCYD t-shirts, other democratic goodnies, access to explusive YDSC events and deals, and much more. The majority of your annual dues go straight into our Chapter Fund to support our organizing here in Kershaw County.
